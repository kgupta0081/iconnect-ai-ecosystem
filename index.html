Management Summary

What Are We Building in 60–75 Days?

A focused, integrated, high-value market-ready product experience using shared platform capabilities across four specialized products.

01
💪
Strengthen What Already Exists
Enhance existing prototype capabilities into production-ready, polished workflows with proper UX and data models.
02
🔨
Build Missing High-Value Capabilities
Identify and build the specific capabilities that complete end-to-end school workflows and create real value for schools.
03
🔗
Connect All Four Products
Build a shared platform layer so all four products share data, authentication, users and core services seamlessly.
04
🚀
Convert to Demo-Ready Ecosystem
QA, security, demo data, and go-to-market readiness to take the portfolio from prototype to school demonstration stage.
